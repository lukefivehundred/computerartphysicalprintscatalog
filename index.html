Herbert W. Franke

Artist Proofs — Computer-Generated Serigraphs, c. 1970

Herbert W. Franke (1927–2022) was a pioneering figure in computer art, moving from early analogue oscilloscope experiments in the 1950s to digital plotter graphics in the late 1960s. The works presented here are artist’s proofs of serigraphs produced around 1970 from his foundational series. These proofs stand outside the numbered editions of 100 and represent rare, primary material from the earliest period of computer-generated graphic art. Catalogued examples reside in major collections including the ZKM | Center for Art and Media Karlsruhe and the Anne + Michael Spalter Digital Art Collection.

Algebraische Kurven (Line Oscillation)

KAES Series — Kurven, AESthetische (Aesthetic Curves)

Date
c. 1970 (serigraph); original plotter drawings 1969
Medium
Serigraph (screenprint) on paper, after digital plotter drawing. Artist’s Proof.
Edition
Artist’s Proof (outside the numbered edition of 100)
Dimensions
Approx. 72.8 × 58 cm (sheet; based on ZKM catalogued examples)
Technique
First digitally produced graphics by Franke. Program written by Peter Henne on a Siemens 4004. Algebraic curves generated and manipulated through step-by-step superpositions and transformations (enlargement, lengthening, reduction), then realized as plotter drawings and transferred to serigraph.
Provenance & Cataloguing
Equivalent numbered impressions are held in the ZKM collection (copies 69 and 70 of the edition of 100).
Catalogued by the Spalter Digital Art Collection as part of the KAES Series, dated 1970, edition of 100.
Exhibited: ART IN / ART OUT: Computer Aided Graphics, Ukrainian Institute of Modern Art, Chicago, 1 February – 16 March 1980, curated by William J. Kolomyjec (Dr. Bill). This was one of the earliest major U.S. survey exhibitions of computer art, featuring pioneers including Franke, Vera Molnár, and Ken Knowlton.
References: ZKM catalogue entry · Spalter Digital

Algebraische Kurven (Line Oscillation #2)

KAES Series — Kurven, AESthetische (Aesthetic Curves)

Date
c. 1970 (serigraph); original plotter drawings 1969
Medium
Serigraph (screenprint) on paper, after digital plotter drawing. Artist’s Proof.
Edition
Artist’s Proof (outside the numbered edition of 100)
Dimensions
Approx. 72.8 × 58 cm (sheet; based on ZKM catalogued examples)
Technique
Variation within the KAES series. Digital generation of algebraic curves via the program by Peter Henne (Siemens 4004), with successive transformations producing dense, overlapping linear structures, realized as serigraph after plotter output.
Provenance & Cataloguing
Equivalent numbered impressions catalogued at ZKM | Center for Art and Media Karlsruhe (edition of 100).
Spalter Digital Art Collection: KAES Series, 1970, edition of 100.
References: ZKM catalogue entry · Spalter Digital

Elektronische Grafik (Oscillogram #2)

Dance of the Electrons Series (from earlier analogue oscillograms)

Date
c. 1970 (serigraph); original analogue images mid-1950s / early 1960s
Medium
Serigraph (screenprint) on paper, after photograph of cathode-ray oscillograph display. Artist’s Proof.
Edition
Artist’s Proof (outside the numbered edition of 100)
Dimensions
Approx. 72.8 × 58 cm (sheet; based on ZKM catalogued examples)
Technique
From Franke’s early analogue computer experiments (device built with Franz Raimann). Curves calculated and displayed on a cathode-ray oscillograph; photographed (often with camera movement to fan the lines). These photographic records formed the basis for the 1970 serigraph editions, including the “Dance of the Electrons” group.
Provenance & Cataloguing
Equivalent numbered impression held at ZKM (edition of 100).
Spalter Digital Art Collection: dated 1970, catalogued from the Dance of the Electrons Series, edition of 100. Related works also appear under Pendular Oscillogram Series.
References: ZKM catalogue entry · Spalter Digital
